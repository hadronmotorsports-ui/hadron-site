    The team

    Six disciplines.
    One car.

    Every Formula Student car needs the same disciplines to come together. Pick one to see what it does.

    Vehicle dynamics

    Suspension geometry, tyre behaviour, kinematics and setup. This group decides how the car turns every driver input into grip.

    Simulation and track testing run side by side, so a setup change is tested against data and not opinion.

    Powertrain

    The engine or motor, the drivetrain and everything that moves power from the source to the tyres.

    Reliability comes first. A car that finishes endurance beats a faster one that does not.

    Aerodynamics

    Wings, undertray and bodywork. Aero turns airflow into downforce that works harder the faster the car goes.

    Every surface is justified by a number, and checked by a simulation before anything is cut.

    Chassis and structure

    The frame or monocoque that carries the driver and every other system, built to the rules and to the lightest weight that stays safe.

    Stiffness, crash structures and manufacturability are all balanced here.

    Electronics and data

    Wiring, sensors, the data logger and the dashboard. This group gives the rest of the team the measurements they need.

    If the car cannot be measured, it cannot be improved.

    Business and media

    Sponsorship, finance, outreach and the design events. The car only gets built when the funding arrives.

    This group also runs the story of the team: the photos, the films and this website.
